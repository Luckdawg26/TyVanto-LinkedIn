Advisory capabilities

  • Decision-ready close and reporting
  • Forecasting and cash discipline
  • Fractional CFO leadership
  • Financing readiness and internal decision support
  • M&A readiness, diligence support, and integration planning

How an engagement begins

  1. A confidential 25-minute Finance Readiness Conversation.
  2. A focused review of close, reporting, forecast, cash, controls, and finance cadence.
  3. A prioritized 90-day roadmap and a defined advisory path.

Who it is for

The practice is best suited to CEOs, boards, operating partners, and finance leaders facing a reporting reset, cash pressure, finance-leadership gap, lender or board pressure, or an approaching financing or transaction event.

Professional boundaries

The CFO Signal Room provides advisory and readiness work only. It does not provide capital placement, buyer sourcing, transaction execution, broker-dealer, legal, tax, attest, or investment-adviser services.
